						Private is the default. When you want a payment on the record, you make it public, and Nulo labels it
						so you always know which is which. A few tokens only work in public. Nulo tells you before you send.
					

				

			


			

				

					
02 three things, plainly

					
What Nulo does for you.

					

						

							yours
							
Your money stays with you.

							

								Nobody holds it for you. Your keys live on your computer, protected by your password or your
								passkey, and Nulo helps you keep them safe.
							

						

						

							your call
							
You decide what's seen.

							
Private by default. Public when you say so. Nulo always shows you which is which.

						

						

							in control
							
Apps have to ask.

							

								When a website wants to do something with your wallet, Nulo shows you exactly what it wants, in
								plain words, before anything happens. You say yes or no.
							

						

					

				

			


			

				

					

						
03 the whole recording

						
Here's everything the public gets. Go ahead and read it.

						

							This is the shape of the record the blockchain keeps of your activity: proof that things happened,
							and no way to tell what, except the entries you chose to make public. Those are there in plain
							words, because you wanted them to be. It's the one place on this page we let the technical words
							in.
