Warn when the fee gives the sender away

A private send on Aztec hides who sent it — unless the fee is paid from your own public Fee Juice, which writes your address next to the transaction. This plan makes the Send page pick a fee source that matches the privacy of the transfer, and when it can't, says so in one line. Nothing appears when things are already right.

Audit status: approved by both reviewers Fable: conditional approve. Codex: five rejects — the last on a single narrow finding — every one checked against the code and folded in. Codex then confirmed its last blocker closed and approved rev 6, and approved your order change in rev 7. You approved the plan on 2026-09-18.

What you'll see

One new row under the fee picker, only on a send that starts from your private balance and pays from your own public Fee Juice:

Your address pays this fee This send hides the amount and the recipient, but the fee names your account publicly. Anyone watching the chain learns this account sent something, and when. Get private gas
private → private
Your address pays this fee The recipient and amount on this send are already public. Paying from public Fee Juice adds your address to them, and the whole transfer becomes readable as yours. Get private gas
private → public

Confirm stays enabled — it's a warning, not a block. No badge, no "you're private ✓" confirmation, no change to the dropdown. The "This send publishes" ledger is a recorded follow-up, not part of this.

How the wallet picks

Sending fromIt tries, in orderNotice?
Private balancePrivate Fee Juice → your Fee Juice → SponsoredOnly on your Fee Juice
Public balanceYour Fee Juice → Private Fee Juice → SponsoredNever — the transfer already names you

The payer that matches the origin is always tried first and Sponsored last — your call at the approval gate. Sponsored never shows a notice: the sponsor contract, not you, is the public fee payer.

Flip the From toggle and the fee source follows. If you pick a source by hand, Send remembers it per account and per origin — separately from what dApp approval windows remember.

The careful part

The wallet only falls back to your public Fee Juice by itself when it has actually read your private gas balance as zero — and opening Send with a private origin now forces a fresh read, so an old cached zero can't trigger it. (The audits found the wallet's balance reader didn't fully honour "fresh" when another screen was reading at the same moment; that small fix ships with this.) If the read fails or is unknown, the wallet skips your Fee Juice: it takes a Sponsored option when one exists, and otherwise picks nothing and says "Couldn't check your private gas. Pick a fee source to continue." A privacy wallet shouldn't choose the revealing option on a guess.

Accepted gap: private gas that arrives while Send is already open isn't noticed until you reopen it. The warning still shows, so nothing leaks silently.

Novelty, blast radius, irreversibility, migration cost and external coupling are all low — one card, one page, one new storage key, no migration (pre-production). What the audits changed

  • "We don't know" is not "zero". The first draft fell back to the public payer whenever private gas wasn't confirmed. Now it needs a positive zero, freshly read.
  • A listed fee contract proves nothing. Discovery failures are swallowed silently, so the rule keys off the balance read, not the list.
  • Selection as a calculation. Three rounds of race conditions (account switches mid-load, flips during init) disappeared once nothing mutates the Send selection.
  • A deleted sponsor stays deleted. The card now remembers edits made while it's open, so a later background refresh can't bring back a fee contract you just removed.
  • Separate memory. Send's remembered pick has its own storage key; you chose this.
