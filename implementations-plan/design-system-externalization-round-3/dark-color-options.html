My read

tertiary (★) makes the masked dots recede nicely and de-emphasizes the mono field-names below their labels — the intended hierarchy. The risk: at 30% opacity (dark) / 35% black (light) the mono values get quite faint — if you want those metadata values to stay comfortably readable, secondary is the safer muted level. A third option: split them — tertiary for the dots, secondary for the metadata values (best of both, two tokens). Tell me which and I'll lock P2.
