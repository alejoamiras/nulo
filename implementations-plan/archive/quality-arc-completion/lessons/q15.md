## Codex consult (xhigh, session 019eeb28)
Verdict: extraction justified; two refinements adopted:
1. **Narrow the seam** — helper takes *pre-loaded rows*, NOT a `load` callback, so `ensureInitialized()` + `this.lock` stay visibly caller-owned (can't be misused before init / outside lock).
