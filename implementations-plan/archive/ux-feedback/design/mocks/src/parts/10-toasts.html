Toasts appear where nobody is looking, and vanish

Toast primitiveHistory listBottom nav
Your noteMost of the time I was not able to read the toasts or noticed them. They were on the upper end of the wallet and I was (for example) looking at a transaction on the transaction list when it said transaction submitted.

What the code does

  • One toast at a time, 12px from the top, centered over the header (ToastManagerBase.vue). Default 2 s; errors 4 s (composables/toast.ts). No aria-live, so screen readers never hear it.
  • "Transaction submitted" fires when the send finishes proving and reaches the network (send-submit.ts), often 30 s or more after you clicked, by which time you're watching the row in History.
  • Every toast looks the same: copy confirmations, "Contact added", failures.

What the research says

  • Most systems put transient messages at the bottom: Material's snackbar, Shopify Polaris, Atlassian's flags. NN/g reports a user missing a bottom message that faded after 5 s (NN/g).
  • "Design assuming toasts will be missed" (Roselli): anything important also has to live somewhere persistent. For a wallet, that's the activity row.
  • WCAG: people must be able to extend timed content (2.2.1), and status messages must be announced (4.1.3). Errors should not disappear on their own.

What changes

  • Messages rise from just above the nav. Successes stay 6 s and pause while hovered or focused; the thin bar shows the time left. Errors stay until dismissed.
  • Transaction messages say what happened and to whom, and carry View, which opens that transaction.
  • Announced to screen readers: successes politely, errors assertively.

Clicks

View → the transaction's detail. Clicking elsewhere leaves it running out.

Effort: small. One primitive in @nulo/design and its call sites' durations.
