"Fee source" and "Sponsored Fee Juice"

Send screendApp transaction windowHome fee balances
Tester, via your note"What the fudge is sponsored fee juice? And why is it called 'Fee source'?" — I think we can just put "Fee" (on the send pop-up at least; I don't recall what the transaction pop-up said, but "fee source" is too explicit). And saying it's "Sponsored", no need of "Sponsored fee juice".

What the code does

  • The dropdown label is "Fee Source" (FeeMethodSelector.vue). Its value is the fee method's name, and the sponsor is literally named "Sponsored Fee Juice" (SPONSORED_FPC_DEFAULT_NAME, wallet/services/fpc/service.ts:45). The public method is just "Fee Juice".
  • The dApp transaction window embeds the same card (OperationCard.vue:298), so one change fixes both. That card's other two states already say "Pay fee with" (locked by the app, or embedded) — three names for one idea.
  • While sponsored, the card still shows "Estimated network fee ~0.0024 FJ", which reads as a cost you pay.
  • Home shows "Public Juice" next to "Private Fee Juice" (GasBalanceCard.vue:161,170); the method row mixes "Fee Juice" and "FJ".

What others do

  • Mainstream wallets say "Network fee" and use the gas token only as the unit (MetaMask, Coinbase, Phantom).
  • Account-abstraction stacks don't label sponsorship at all; the fee simply shows as free — "your users just see your product working" (Pimlico).
  • Starknet's Ready exposes the fee-token choice (STRK vs ETH) only on a second screen, opt-in (Ready).

What changes

  • Label "Fee Source" → "Fee". Sponsor name "Sponsored Fee Juice" → "Sponsored"; "Fee Juice" → "Public Fee Juice" so the pair reads symmetric.
  • "Estimated network fee" → "You pay", and it says Nothing when sponsored.
  • The menu's right column shows what each option can spend (your balance, or "free") instead of repeating public/private.
  • Same words on Home ("Public Fee Juice") and "FJ" everywhere as the unit.

Clicks

Unchanged. The dApp window gets the same card for free.

Effort: copy in 4 files + the seeded FPC name. No migration (pre-production).

My pick: A

Your version, plus the thing it misses: with a sponsor, the card still quotes a fee, which is the bigger source of "what is this?". A is copy-only and fixes both hosts at once. Take B if you'd rather have one phrase ("Pay fee with") across send and dApp windows. C is the calmest screen but the most work; worth it only if item 8 needs the space.

Confidence: moderate — A vs B is taste; the "You pay: Nothing" line is the part I'd defend.
