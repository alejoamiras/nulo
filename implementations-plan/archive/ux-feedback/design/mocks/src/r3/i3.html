Round 3You like the strikethrough and asked what I think. Keep it, and strike only the dollars.

The case against it

  • A struck price usually means a discount. Nothing here is on sale: the fee is simply someone else's to pay.
  • Most screen readers don't announce a strikethrough, so the line is read as "You pay nothing, about 3.58 FJ". The row needs its own spoken text: "You pay nothing. The sponsor covers about $0.215."
  • As drawn in round 2 it's the busiest line in the matrix: six struck decimals of FJ, then the dollars.

Why it's still the better line

  • A crossed-out fee beside "nothing" is how shops show waived shipping. People already know it, so the line needs no "sponsor" and no tooltip, which matches your call on item 9.
  • Like V3, it stays on one line at every fee below.
  • Striking only the dollars (V4′) fixes the busy part. FJ appears only when there's no live price, the same fallback V3 uses.

My pick: V4′

It's your strikethrough with one figure struck instead of two. The fee still reads as waived at a glance, and the six-decimal FJ amount goes, since nobody reads it when they aren't paying it. The spoken text goes with either version.
