02 three things, plainly

  
What Nulo does for you.

  

    
yours
Your money stays with you.
Nobody holds it for you. Your keys live on your computer, protected by your password or your passkey, and Nulo helps you keep them safe.

    
your call
You decide what's seen.
Private is the default: how much you have and who you pay stay between you and them. Want a payment on the record? Make it public, on purpose, and Nulo shows you which is which.

    
in control
Apps have to ask.
When a website wants to do something with your wallet, Nulo shows you exactly what it wants, in plain words, before anything happens. You say yes or no.

Designer's notes (not part of the page)

  

    
Legibility rule
Nothing that must be read sits on grain. The nav is a solid bar; the headline, both buttons and every label live on a plate (solid ground, one hairline, bracket corners so it still reads as the camera's UI). The grain is ambience; the plates are the page.
Body type is Inter at 18px on the plate, headline is Space Grotesk 54px. Mono is reserved for the camera's own labels, which are allowed to be small because they are flavour.

    
Copy rule
Above the fold: no "notes", no "proofs", no "nullifier", no "self-custody". Say what it means for a person. The technical words appear once, inside the recording panel, where their unreadability is the argument.

Page shape
Hero feed → "same moment, two screens" → three plain promises → the recording → the preview notice → CTA, as one continuous wall: no rules between the numbered steps, the grain runs through, only the plates interrupt it. The numbers stay because they are the order a first-time visitor needs the information.

    
Motion budget
The feed, the blinking REC, the clock, the tracking box, the recording stream, and a faint monitor-wall grain behind every section (8 fps, very low contrast) so the page never sits on empty black. Cursor pans the camera in the hero only. Reduced motion: still feed, still grain, no blink.

    
Claims kept honest
The page now says "you choose what it sees": private by default, public by choice, labelled either way, which is what the wallet does. "Your money stays with you" replaced "not ours" (it read as if we were a counterparty). Password or passkey, testnet, no audit, Chrome and Firefox: all true as of today's build.

    
Build
The renderer is the same 120 lines as the study frame (value noise, Bayer 4×4, five-glyph ramp) at the real viewport width; columns are computed after fonts load. No canvas, no library. apps/landing only.
