6. `LOW` I did not find the same async-init anti-pattern in `useFormState` at [useFormState.ts:82](../../packages/extension/src/composables/useFormState.ts:82) or `useEntityCrud` at [useEntityCrud.ts:55](../../packages/extension/src/composables/useEntityCrud.ts:55). `useFormState` is synchronous, and `useEntityCrud` exposes `isLoading`/`error` rather than silently swallowing action preconditions.

Recommended preventive measure: add an explicit `isReady` computed to every async-init popup/window, require all primary/secondary actions to use `:disabled="!isReady || isLoading"`, and make handlers fail closed with `if (!isReady.value) throw new Error("action called before ready")` instead of silent `return`. That fixes production behavior and turns future races into loud test failures.
