Verdict: **audit-3 was wrong; the likely cliff is wallet-side full-store `chrome.storage.local` scans and lock contention in `DappSessionService`, not the dApp’s 2s key-exchange timeout.**

1. `addDappSession()` itself is cheap, but it always calls `deleteExpired()` first at [service.ts:114](../../packages/extension/src/wallet/services/dapp-session/service.ts:114). `deleteExpired()` does `this.storage.getValues()` at [service.ts:314](../../packages/extension/src/wallet/services/dapp-session/service.ts:314), and `EntityStorage.getValues()` calls `chrome.storage.local.get()` with no key at [entity_storage.ts:125](../../packages/wallet-core/src/storage/entity_storage.ts:125). That loads the entire local storage area, not just dapp sessions.

2. `tryGetDappSessionByOriginAndChain()` does the same whole-store scan again at [service.ts:91](../../packages/extension/src/wallet/services/dapp-session/service.ts:91). There is no decrypt / argon2 / passhash work here: `getActiveProfile()` is just `SessionManager.getActive()` on in-memory/session state at [profile/service.ts:87](../../packages/extension/src/wallet/services/profile/service.ts:87) and [session-manager.ts:151](../../packages/extension/src/wallet/services/profile/session-manager.ts:151).

3. `setCapabilityGrants()` and `setVerificationHash()` are keyed get/set only at [service.ts:234](../../packages/extension/src/wallet/services/dapp-session/service.ts:234) and [service.ts:192](../../packages/extension/src/wallet/services/dapp-session/service.ts:192). No encrypted storage, no KDF, no IndexedDB.

4. Verify popup creation is raw `chrome.windows.create(...)` at [background.ts:165](../../packages/extension/src/wallet/services/wallet-sdk/background.ts:165). It is not wrapped in `WindowManager`, not awaited, and has no rate limiter. The only queue here is the generic FIFO `Lock` at [lock.ts:19](../../packages/wallet-core/src/utils/lock.ts:19), so if the whole-store scans are slow, the later keyed writes queue behind them.

5. Re-evaluation: the 2s theory is ruled out as the dominant cause. Iter #9’s regression was noise or side-effect, not confirmation. Iter #14’s no-effect means the real 30s cliff is on the wallet side before `chrome.windows.create(/verify)`.
