Verdict: **the verify popup is not waiting on PXE; shard 5 is tripping the wallet-sdk’s hidden `2s` key-exchange deadline before `chrome.windows.create(/verify)` is ever reached.**

1. The local verify path has no PXE/readiness gate. After `approveDiscover`, the only local awaits before `chrome.windows.create(...)` are dApp-session storage calls: `addDappSession()` + `setCapabilityGrants()` before `approveDiscovery()` at [background.ts:396](../../packages/extension/src/wallet/services/wallet-sdk/background.ts:396), then `tryGetDappSessionByOriginAndChain()` + `setVerificationHash()` before opening verify at [background.ts:152](../../packages/extension/src/wallet/services/wallet-sdk/background.ts:152). I found no `pxeService.isReady()`, `awaitPxe`, or block-sync call on this path.

2. The real gate is upstream ECDH handshake timing. The dApp side starts secure-channel setup at [wallet.ts:89](../../packages/playground/src/lib/wallet.ts:89), and `@aztec/wallet-sdk` hard-fails it after `2000ms` at [extension_provider.ts:20](../../node_modules/@aztec/wallet-sdk/src/extension/provider/extension_provider.ts:20) and [extension_provider.ts:62](../../node_modules/@aztec/wallet-sdk/src/extension/provider/extension_provider.ts:62). The wallet side must, within that budget, process `KEY_EXCHANGE_REQUEST`, run `generateKeyPair` / `importPublicKey` / `deriveSessionKeys`, and fire `onSessionEstablished` at [background_connection_handler.ts:277](../../node_modules/@aztec/wallet-sdk/src/extension/handlers/background_connection_handler.ts:277).

3. If that 2s budget is missed, your fixture waits 30s for a popup that will never exist. The playground swallows the handshake failure and only flips UI state to `error` at [connect.ts:35](../../packages/playground/src/sections/connect.ts:35). That exactly fits `awaitVerifyPopup` timing out while the underlying error is earlier.

4. `chrome.windows.create` backpressure is not the primary issue here. On the failing path it is never called unless `onSessionEstablished` completes; that call site is [background.ts:165](../../packages/extension/src/wallet/services/wallet-sdk/background.ts:165).

5. First-test-on-shard-5 points to cold-worker timing, not cumulative Aztec state. A cold or restarted SW must finish `BarretenbergSync.initSingleton`, migrations, and `services.start()` before `initWalletSdkHandler()` is wired at [runtime.ts:92](../../packages/extension/src/wallet/runtime.ts:92) and [runtime.ts:168](../../packages/extension/src/wallet/runtime.ts:168). That is compatible with “sometimes >2s on CI”, and incompatible with blaming PXE on the verify path.

Concrete fix: patch `@aztec/wallet-sdk` to raise or configure `KEY_EXCHANGE_TIMEOUT_MS` at [extension_provider.ts:20](../../node_modules/@aztec/wallet-sdk/src/extension/provider/extension_provider.ts:20), e.g. `2000 -> 10000`. That is the real protocol timeout governing verify-popup creation. If you want a one-line falsifier before changing behavior, surface `lastError` from the playground fixture after discover approval; I expect it to read `Key exchange timeout`.
