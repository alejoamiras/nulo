## Grants and scopes

- **The execution layer's selector refusals interpolate the call.** Six throws start `Scope violation:` and carry the call's name, the ABI function and the target (`apps/extension/src/wallet/services/execution/tx-request-builder.ts:346`, `:595`, `fast-path.ts:139`, `view-executor.ts:372`, `service.ts:1044`, `authwit-discoverer.ts:203`). They run after the grant check, while the wallet builds, simulates or signs, some with no window (a simulation or a utility call), so they reach the executors' failed records and whatever logs the error there: they need their own sink inventory. From [dapp-grants](dapp-grants/plan.md), F-1.
- **Every other failure before a dApp send is claimed reads "Popup closed early".** `failQueuedForError` files anything but a scope refusal as `popup_bound` (`apps/extension/src/wallet/services/wallet-sdk/queued-journal.ts:273`, from `background.ts:1184`), and so does the ended-session path (`background.ts:1139`): a Terms refusal, a missing capability, a malformed request. Their honest labels are a copy decision, and the Terms case is next in line (2026-09-29, decided under the owner's delegation). From [dapp-grants](dapp-grants/plan.md), F-2.
- **A dApp that cannot read the accounts can test whether an address is in the session**, through any scoped call's success versus refusal: with a contracts grant, `isTokenRegistered(token, { scopes: [candidate] })` answers a member and refuses anyone else, with no window (`packages/wallet-bridge/src/method-descriptors.ts:157`, `scope-enforcement.ts:67-86`, `dispatcher.ts:904`; `apps/extension/src/wallet/services/wallet-sdk/background.ts:203-208`), and `simulateTx` with `opts.from` or `scopes` does the same. Closing it is a design choice (for example, without `canGet` a named account is refused whether or not it is a member), for its own plan. From [dapp-grants](dapp-grants/plan.md), F-3.
- **A dApp cannot widen a contract-classes grant.** Coverage is type-only (`packages/wallet-bridge/src/dispatcher.ts:719-720`), so a wider request opens no window unless the type was rejected before (`:514`). The answer shows the held classes, truthfully, but the dApp has no way to ask for more; a field-aware coverage rule is a product change for its own plan. From [dapp-grants](dapp-grants/plan.md), F-4.
- **Repeated scope refusals pile up in Activity.** Every scope-refused send leaves one failed row, which the GC never evicts (`apps/extension/src/wallet/services/operation-journal/gc.ts:17-24`, `:127-130`), and the per-session cap of 8 counts only `queued` rows (`apps/extension/src/wallet/services/wallet-sdk/queued-journal.ts:37`, `:176-187`), so a connected app that retries a refused send adds one card per try, with no limit. Cap or merge repeated refusals per session; on the 2026-09-29 panel, codex would have blocked the sign-off on it. From [dapp-grants](dapp-grants/plan.md), F-5.

## Wallet safety

- **The address-keyed fee maps outlive a deleted profile off the live path.** `nulo:ui:feePaymentMethods` and `nulo:ui:sendFeePaymentMethods` hold `{ [address]: … }` (`apps/extension/src/popup/components/modules/send/FeeSettingsCard.vue:293-296`, and `withSendSlot` in `fee-send-selection.ts` beside it). Only the reset page removes them (`apps/extension/src/popup/pages/settings/security/reset.vue:85-87`), after the live delete returns, so the resume and torn-reap paths, and a popup closed mid-delete, keep the deleted profile's addresses. Pruning by address is unsafe, since two profiles can share one, and moving the global wipe onto the other paths changes their behaviour: it needs its own decision. From [wallet-safety-fixes](wallet-safety-fixes/plan.md), F-1.
- **An Allow the watchdog displaced mid-un-hide leaves receipts hidden under `trusted`.** After a stall of five minutes or more inside the lock, a displaced Allow with no Block after it stops un-hiding, and only `setTrustAllow` writes `hidden: false` (`unhideLocked`, `apps/extension/src/wallet/services/incoming-transfer/service.ts:659-675`), so nothing un-hides the rest. The owner signed the state off as a residual; a `(BUG PIN)` in `service.scenarios.test.ts` holds it. Candidates: re-run the un-hide on replay, or un-hide before the trust write, which needs a `pending` trust row first because the floor skips a contract with none (`:865`). From [wallet-safety-fixes](wallet-safety-fixes/plan.md), F-4.
- **A pin or a token-deletion cleanup racing a profile deletion can recreate its pinned-tokens key** (residual R1, signed off by the owner). The purge removes `nulo:ui:pinnedTokens@<id>` on every deletion path, but a popup that has not yet seen the deletion can write it again (`pin` and `onTokenDeleted`, `apps/extension/src/composables/usePinnedTokens.ts:214-243`). A restore that reuses the id then shows those pins (`apps/extension/src/wallet/services/profile/service.ts:1526-1529` frees the id; `operation-journal/service.ts:266-267` says why ids come back); otherwise the key stays as an orphan. Fixes: fence the key by incarnation, or a sweep cheaper than reading the whole store at every background start. From [wallet-safety-fixes](wallet-safety-fixes/plan.md), F-5.
- **Two of the token add's compensations delete by id without lock ownership.** `persistToken`'s post-set deletion and network checks (`apps/extension/src/wallet/services/token/service.ts:419-431`) delete `token.id` even when the lock's watchdog displaced the add, so a same-id restore's row can go with it; the last check before `onTokenAdded` deletes only while the add owns the lock. At those two points the row may postdate the purge's snapshot, so skipping the delete can orphan it: the choice needs its own decision. From [wallet-safety-fixes](wallet-safety-fixes/plan.md), F-7.
- **Recovery phrase and Change password have no submit latch.** A mouse double-click on Retrieve runs `handleUnlock` twice (`apps/extension/src/popup/pages/settings/security/export/seed.vue:55-67`), and the second `countdown.start()` leaves a 5-minute close that "keep open" cannot cancel (`apps/extension/src/composables/useSecretCountdown.ts:26-35`). Change Password is disabled while loading (`apps/extension/src/popup/pages/settings/security/change-password.vue:228`), so only a same-tick double click reaches it twice. `seed.vue` also has no unmount fence (`full.vue`'s `generation` is the pattern), so a Retrieve that resolves after the page left sets the phrase on the dead page and arms that timer. Since both buttons refuse a repeat Enter, only a pointer reaches either twice. From [keyboard-guards](keyboard-guards/plan.md), FU-1.
- **Verify's OK accepts a repeat or composing Enter** (`apps/extension/src/popup/windows/verify/index.vue:221`, handler `:75-80`). It neither sends nor signs, so CLAUDE.md's rule does not bind it, but it records "Always trust". `connect-window` rewrites this window and applies `refuseRepeatEnter` there when it merges. From [keyboard-guards](keyboard-guards/plan.md), FU-2.
- **Two keyboard focus rings do not show.** The sub-page back arrow is a bare `<button>` (`packages/design/src/ui/SubPageHeaderBase.vue:36-45`) under the base stylesheet's `button { outline: none; }` (`packages/design/src/base.css:270-275`), on every page built on `SubPageHeader` or `CollapsingHeroLayout`, and onboarding's active method tab draws its `:focus-visible` outline in `--nulo-accent` over a fill of the same colour (`apps/extension/src/onboarding/pages/create.vue:253-261`). A keyboard user cannot see that focus is on Back. Drawing a ring changes how those screens look, so it is the owner's call. From [keyboard-guards](keyboard-guards/plan.md), FU-3.
- **Send's token card has no focus style of its own.** It is a `role="button"` row with `tabindex="0"` and no `:focus-visible` rule (`apps/extension/src/popup/components/modules/send/SelectTokenCard.vue:92-93`), so Tab shows only the browser's default outline, on the failed card's Retry too. It predates send-states; a designed ring is an owner call. From [send-states](send-states/plan.md).

## Amounts, sends and fees

- **Six capped amounts guess their decimals.** For an unknown token or a dApp mint they assume 0 and print a figure off by 10^decimals: `apps/extension/src/popup/components/modules/activity/TransactionCard.vue:41` and `:48-58`, `apps/extension/src/popup/pages/tx/[id].vue:83` and `:89-97`, `apps/extension/src/popup/pages/received/[id].vue:105`, `apps/extension/src/popup/components/popups/IncomingTrustPopup.vue:62-63`. They keep the plain cut, since the compact form would show a dApp's mint of one 18-decimal token as ">999T". From [ux-owner-picks](ux-owner-picks/plan.md), O1.
- **The authwit popups keep the 60 s ceiling.** `revokeAuthwits` and `setRegistryEnabled` await a full proof through `AuthRegistryServiceClient`, which sets no per-method timeout (`apps/extension/src/popup/components/popups/RevokeAuthwitsPopup.vue:103`, `ChangeAuthwitsRegistryPopup.vue:59`), so on Firefox without Presto they report a failure the background does not have. `ExecutionServiceClient`'s `getRequestTimeoutMs` override is the fix; what the popups show while they wait is an owner call. From [failed-send-check](failed-send-check/plan.md), F-1.
- **A definitive "won't go through".** DROPPED cannot prove a send never reached the network, so a send the check cannot confirm ends "Unconfirmed". An Aztec tx carries an expiry after which no block includes it; a check that knows it and the chain's time could say "won't go through, you can send it again". Unverified: the field's name and shape at Aztec 5.2.0. From [failed-send-check](failed-send-check/plan.md), F-2.
- **A checked dApp send can leave its public authwit out of the revoke index.** When a dApp send's `sentTxRecorder` never ran (`apps/extension/src/wallet/services/execution/dapp-send-executor.ts:516`), its live public authwit is missing from the index the revoke UI reads (`auth-registry/service.ts`, `recordPendingAuthwits`). Pre-existing; the check makes it visible by writing `sent`. From [failed-send-check](failed-send-check/plan.md), F-3.
- **A chain prune after inclusion is caught by no send path.** A mined receipt at `PROPOSED` or `CHECKPOINTED` is "went through" for the check as for `TransactionService.waitForTx`, and blocks at those statuses can be pruned; the wallet already treats the checkpointed tip as prunable for incoming records (`orphanedByReconciliation`). Every "went through" would need a re-read until finalized, with its own copy. Unverified: what a node answers for a pruned tx's hash. From [failed-send-check](failed-send-check/plan.md), F-4.
- **A failed card beside a settled row for the same send.** A failed record whose transaction also has a settled activity row shows both cards, with the same outcome. The review panel asks to hide the failed one when a settled row carries its hash; Codex would have withheld the blanket sign-off on it. From [failed-send-check](failed-send-check/plan.md), F-5.
- **A neutral snack style for "Send not confirmed".** The snack keeps the error kind's red icon, which reads as a failure while the wallet still checks (`apps/extension/src/popup/pages/send-submit.ts`). An owner UI call. From [failed-send-check](failed-send-check/plan.md), F-6.
- **The status colours' contrast in the light theme.** The gray, amber and green subtitles of the activity card and the journal page, raised by the review panel. From [failed-send-check](failed-send-check/plan.md), F-7.
- **A node's refusal at the send line reads "Not confirmed yet" for 30 minutes.** An unfunded sponsor or a fee per gas below the base fee makes the node throw `Invalid tx: <reason>`, a per-call JSON-RPC error on an HTTP 200 that the transport never retries; the row fails from `submitting` with its hash and ends "Unconfirmed" (pinned in `apps/extension/src/wallet/services/execution/service.composition.test.ts`). A POST retried after a lost answer can also come back refused for a send the node took, so ending a refused row "failed" at once needs the send's transport to report an earlier failed attempt, and copy for it. Related: the sponsor funding check above. From [failed-send-check](failed-send-check/plan.md), F-8.
- **A checked record shows no tx hash or explorer link.** The owner's standing call is "ALWAYS link the tx hash where an explorer URL exists" ([incoming-public-transfers](incoming-public-transfers/plan.md), decision L21); the journal page of a failed send links neither. From [failed-send-check](failed-send-check/plan.md), F-9.
- **Home's error snack covers the row above the tab bar.** It sits 12 px above the footer and stays until closed, so it covers the lower half of an awaiting card's cancel × there; after a background restart that card is the reported send's until the restarted background reaps its row. From [failed-send-check](failed-send-check/plan.md), F-10.
- **The Revoke authorizations and authwit registry popups never check the sponsor.** They mount the fee card with no estimate (`apps/extension/src/popup/components/popups/RevokeAuthwitsPopup.vue:207`, `ChangeAuthwitsRegistryPopup.vue:111`) and send with its settings, so a short sponsor still proves there and the node refuses it, with nothing spent. Covering them needs an estimate in each popup, or a probe outside the estimate. From [send-states](send-states/plan.md), F-1.
- **A dApp's embedded sponsor payment is never checked.** A wallet-sdk dApp's `embeddedFeePayment: "fpc"` (`apps/extension/src/wallet/services/execution/operation-planner.ts:95`) is estimated by `EmbeddedStrategy`, not `FpcStrategy`, and the execute window hides the fee card behind "Fee payment method set by …" (`apps/extension/src/popup/windows/execute/OperationCard.vue:296`), so a dry shared sponsor proves and is refused there. Covering it needs a probe in that estimate and a notice on that row, an owner UI decision. From [send-states](send-states/plan.md), F-2.
- **The dApp window offers no way to get fee juice when nothing can pay.** Outside Send the nudge needs a selected method with a confirmed zero (`apps/extension/src/popup/components/modules/send/FeeSettingsCard.vue:277-298`), and a short sponsor leaves none selected: an account with no gas sees "Select method", the sponsor notice and a menu of disabled rows, where Send shows Get Fee Juice. - **A re-enabled Sponsored row reads "free" before it is checked again.** Once the transaction changes, a sponsor just found short gets its row back with Nulo's usual label (`apps/extension/src/popup/components/modules/send/fee-helpers.ts:216-228`), though nothing has re-read its balance. Picking it starts an estimate that checks it again, but a Confirm before that estimate returns skips the check (the entry above). A label for that state is an owner copy call. From [send-states](send-states/plan.md), UI impact row 4.
- **The sponsor notice is not announced.** `fee-sponsor-short` is a plain row with no live region (`apps/extension/src/popup/components/modules/send/FeeSettingsCard.vue:896-901`), so a screen reader is not told that the payer changed. From [send-states](send-states/plan.md).
- **A hand-added sponsor that falls back to Nulo's names the payer by its row title.** The notice reads "The sponsor can't cover this fee right now, so Sponsored pays it."
