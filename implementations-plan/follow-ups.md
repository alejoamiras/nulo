## Grants and scopes

- **The execution layer's selector refusals interpolate the call.** Six throws start `Scope violation:` and carry the call's name, the ABI function and the target (`apps/extension/src/wallet/services/execution/tx-request-builder.ts:346`, `:595`, `fast-path.ts:139`, `view-executor.ts:372`, `service.ts:1049`, `authwit-discoverer.ts:203`). They run after the grant check, while the wallet builds, simulates or signs, some with no window (a simulation or a utility call), so they reach the executors' failed records and whatever logs the error there: they need their own sink inventory. From [dapp-grants](archive/dapp-grants/plan.md), F-1.
- **Every other failure before a dApp send is claimed reads "Popup closed early".** `failQueuedForError` files anything but a scope refusal as `popup_bound` (`apps/extension/src/wallet/services/wallet-sdk/queued-journal.ts:273`, from `background.ts:1228`), and so does the ended-session path (`background.ts:1183`): a Terms refusal, a missing capability, a malformed request. Their honest labels are a copy decision, and the Terms case is next in line (2026-09-29, decided under the owner's delegation). From [dapp-grants](archive/dapp-grants/plan.md), F-2.
- **A dApp that cannot read the accounts can test whether an address is in the session**, through any scoped call's success versus refusal: with a contracts grant, `isTokenRegistered(token, { scopes: [candidate] })` answers a member and refuses anyone else, with no window (`packages/wallet-bridge/src/method-descriptors.ts:157`, `scope-enforcement.ts:67-86`, `dispatcher.ts:904`; `apps/extension/src/wallet/services/wallet-sdk/background.ts:203-208`), and `simulateTx` with `opts.from` or `scopes` does the same. Closing it is a design choice (for example, without `canGet` a named account is refused whether or not it is a member), for its own plan. From [dapp-grants](archive/dapp-grants/plan.md), F-3.
- **A dApp cannot widen a contract-classes grant.** Coverage is type-only (`packages/wallet-bridge/src/dispatcher.ts:719-720`), so a wider request opens no window unless the type was rejected before (`:514`). The answer shows the held classes, truthfully, but the dApp has no way to ask for more; a field-aware coverage rule is a product change for its own plan. From [dapp-grants](archive/dapp-grants/plan.md), F-4.
- **Repeated scope refusals pile up in Activity.** Every scope-refused send leaves one failed row, which the GC never evicts (`apps/extension/src/wallet/services/operation-journal/gc.ts:17-24`, `:127-130`), and the per-session cap of 8 counts only `queued` rows (`apps/extension/src/wallet/services/wallet-sdk/queued-journal.ts:37`, `:176-187`), so a connected app that retries a refused send adds one card per try, with no limit. Cap or merge repeated refusals per session; on the 2026-09-29 panel, codex would have blocked the sign-off on it. From [dapp-grants](archive/dapp-grants/plan.md), F-5.
