Journal reaper fences (batch 8 of the audit remediation)

The wallet keeps a durable journal of every in-flight dApp operation, and a "reaper" that declares abandoned entries failed so the UI never shows a stuck card forever. Three bugs around that machinery:

1 · The reaper cancels ops that are just waiting in line (N-07, the big one)

When a dApp sends several transactions, they queue one behind another while you approve each popup — and each popup can legitimately sit open 10 minutes. But the reaper assumed any entry still "queued" after 10 minutes was dead, so request #3 could get auto-cancelled while you were still approving #1. You'd then approve #3's popup and be told it was cancelled. Fix: the existing 30-second "still alive" heartbeat — which already covers a later stage of the wait — now also covers the waiting-in-line stage, with a hard 90-minute ceiling so a genuinely hung queue still gets cleaned up honestly (never "spared forever"). The reaper also re-checks each entry at the moment of failing it, so an entry that got claimed mid-sweep is left alone.

2 · A wait that could spin forever (N-16)

After revoking a permission, the wallet polls "has the transaction confirmed yet?" every 100 ms — with no time limit. If the wallet locked meanwhile, that poll spun for the whole lock. Now it gives up after 2 minutes with an honest "not confirmed yet (it may still go through)" instead of a misleading generic error.

3 · A small memory leak on cancelled claims (N-25)

When a queued op was cancelled at the exact moment the wallet tried to claim it, an internal abort-controller entry was left behind until the next browser restart. The cleanup now covers that path (both possible keys, always before the slot is released).
