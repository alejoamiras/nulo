Blueprint · mid · Nulo extension · 2026-09-17

Honest token list, quieter sync

When you open a brand-new wallet, Home says “nothing here yet” for up to a minute while the default tokens are quietly being set up — then rows pop in. And a pulsing “catching up” dot shows far more often than it should, next to balances that are actually correct. This plan makes Home tell the truth from the first second, removes the dot, and fixes the machinery underneath.

Mockups you already reviewed: Nulo Holdings Loading States. Full technical plan: implementations-plan/holdings-loading-sync/plan.md in the worktree holdings-loading-sync.

What the two audits changed

Where it ended: Claude reviewer — conditional approve; codex — conditional approve after three rejections, with its two remaining conditions folded in and one left for you (the “—” state below). The first draft did not survive; the plan was rewritten around what both reviewers agreed on:

  • My “skip old history for new wallets” shortcut is gone. Three different designs of it each lost a transfer somewhere — the same address exists on every network sharing an L1, a network can be removed and re-added, and the recovery phrase is on screen before the first account exists. You moved it to the follow-up plan, which fixes the same problem for every wallet without skipping anything.
  • “Sync is failing” must look at the whole attempt. The draft only noticed one kind of failure and would have stayed silent with the node fully down. It now judges every attempt by whether real progress was made.
  • The status line would have cried wolf after unlocking. The clock now starts at the first failure of the current streak, and resets when you lock.
  • Opening the popup could have burned all three setup attempts in seconds when the node is down. Reading status no longer triggers work; a single, latched “make sure setup is running” call does.
  • The total could have stayed hidden forever on a hung token. It now gives up after 12 seconds (your choice) and shows what it knows.
  • Dropped: telling “network hiccup” apart from “chain reorg”. With the dot gone, a needless re-check is invisible and harmless; the safety behaviour that was audited before stays exactly as it is.

Human context

  • Why rows were missing: the wallet only writes a token row after it has booted its proving engine, registered the contract and asked the chain for name, symbol and decimals — three slow questions, one after another, twice on Alpha. Nothing on screen changes until all of that is done.
  • What the dot really meant: “the list of incoming public transfers might be incomplete”. Never “your balance is wrong” — balances are read directly from the chain by a separate path.
  • Why research says remove it: people stop seeing an indicator that is usually on, and one placed next to a number reads as “this number is suspect”. Other wallets show sync status once, and only when a value is in doubt.
  • One honest leftover: on a busy token, a new incoming public transfer can take a long time to appear in activity while the token's old history is read first — for any wallet, new ones included. The balance is right the whole time. You chose to solve that properly in a follow-up plan (incoming-tip-first-scan: newest first, history behind) rather than bolt a shortcut on here.
  • Tooling note: codex cannot read files on this machine (the OS blocks its sandbox; fixing that needs root). Its reviews ran on the plan plus pasted code excerpts; the Claude reviewer read the repo directly.
