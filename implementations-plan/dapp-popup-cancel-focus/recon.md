## Collision / dedup risks

- Do NOT add a second reverse-lookup style: mirror `cancelInteraction`'s linear scan over `storage` (≤ a handful of live interactions) rather than a new index map.
- Do NOT bypass `WindowPort` with raw `chrome.windows.*` from the SW — the fake adapter is what makes `WindowManager` unit-testable.
- The `TransactionAwaitingCard` cancel button sits in the `#actions` slot; a card-level `click` must not fire when the cancel button is clicked (`@click.stop` on the button, pinned by a test).
