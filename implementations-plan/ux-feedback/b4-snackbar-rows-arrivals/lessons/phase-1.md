# Phase 1 · The snack and every call site, atomically

Built:

- `packages/design/src/composables/toast.ts`: `ToastKind`, `ToastAction`, `ToastOptions`,
  `ToastState`, `SUCCESS_TOAST_MS = 6_000`; `openToast({ kind, label, sub?, action? })` (a missing
  or unknown kind lands as `error`), `closeToast`, `holdToast(bool)` (idempotent; a hold on a
  success freezes the remaining time, a release re-arms it; an error has no timer). One module
  singleton, as before. `TOAST_DURATION` and the duration argument are gone.
- `toast.test.ts`: 10 cases, fake timers — visible at 5.9 s, gone at 6 s; hold at 2 s, release,
  the remaining 4 s; repeated hold/release no-ops; error stays after any time; missing and unknown
  kinds are errors; a replacement gets a new id and a fresh timer, and does not inherit a hold;
  `closeToast` clears the hold and is a no-op on nothing; sub and action carried; the singleton.
- `packages/design/src/ui/ToastManagerBase.vue`: `Teleport … defer`; one fixed wrap
  (`bottom: <bottomInset>px`, default 12) with two regions in one grid cell, `role="status"`
  (`aria-live="polite"`) for successes and `role="alert"` for errors; each region a
  `<Transition mode="out-in">` around the card (`data-testid="snackbar"`, `data-kind`), 16px
  `check-circle` (green) or `close-circle` (red), title (`snackbar-title`), optional sub
  (`snackbar-sub`), optional action button (`snackbar-action`), × on errors (`snackbar-close`,
  `aria-label="Close"`). The drawn values: `width: calc(100% - 32px)`, `padding: 12px 14px`,
  surface-high, 1px outline (red on error), the 0 8px 24px shadow, headline 12/700 0.06em
  uppercase title, 11px secondary sub, 11/700 0.12em uppercase accent action (6px 4px), 24×24 ×
  with a −6px right margin. Enter/leave: opacity + 20px rise, 0.15s `var(--bezier)`; reduced
  motion drops the transform.
  - A replacement of another kind, and a close, wait for the current card's leave; `after-leave`
    installs whatever the shared toast holds then (a close during the wait installs nothing).
  - Hover and focus-within hold the timer; both reset on every new card and are re-derived at
    `after-enter` (`:hover` and `document.activeElement`), so a replacement under a resting
    pointer is held too. × returns focus to the element it came from when focus was inside.
  - `onSelect` closes first, then runs the action.

Decisions the plan left open:

1. **The error reachable in smoke is "Couldn't copy address"**, raised by the header copy with
   `navigator.clipboard.writeText` stubbed to reject. The spec stubs the write for every case (to
   resolve or to reject): the snack is the wallet's reaction to the write's outcome, and the real
   clipboard needs a focused document the suite cannot promise on both browsers. The real clipboard
   path stays pinned by `accounts.test.ts`. Settings → Accounts is the no-nav page: the Header
   is there, so no new testid was needed.
2. **`waitForToast` keeps its positional `timeout`** and takes `{ kind }` as a fourth argument, so
   the twelve callers that pass a timeout are untouched.
3. **Classification of the borderline sites** follows S-6 as written: the two `NewNetworkPopup`
   non-activated outcomes ("Finish or cancel your pending transaction to switch to it", "the switch
   didn't confirm") are errors (a refusal, a state to act on); "Contact import canceled" is a
   success; "No contacts found in file" / "No contacts selected for import" are errors; "Token
   added. Couldn't load balance — we'll retry automatically." is a success; the four
   "Fetching … again" / "Record removed" / "The session was interrupted" notices are successes
   (S-5). Result: 63 successes, 67 errors.
4. **The view resets its holds on every new card.** `openToast` clears the composable's hold, so
   a card replaced under a resting pointer would otherwise time out unheld; the view re-reads
   `:hover` and `activeElement` at `after-enter` and reports again.
5. **The comment on the `IncomingTrustPopup` `decide` helper's third parameter went with the
   parameter** (the success icon); the two callers pass the label only.

Held for the owner (not decided here):

- **A snack over a footer CTA on a page without the nav.** With the drawn 12px inset (S-1), a
  success covers the bottom 60px of the export pages for 6 s and an error until closed; the
  agree/continue and download buttons sit there. The suite met it on the account-export →
  full-backup path (above). Nothing was built for it: no extra inset, no auto-close on
  navigation, no pointer pass-through. The owner's S-1/S-3 call decides.
- **A snack that opens under a resting pointer is held from its first frame.** The hold S-12
  asks for is read from `:hover` when the card has entered, so a card that appears under a still
  pointer (a press on a footer button, then the result) waits until the pointer moves. Built as
  the plan's replacement rule implies; whether a first open should start the 6 s regardless is
  the owner's.

Traps:

- Vue's leave under fake timers needs `requestAnimationFrame` faked and `getComputedStyle`
  stubbed for the card's subtree; without the stub jsdom reports no duration and the leave ends
  after two frames, so a 150 ms wait cannot be observed.
- `@vue/test-utils` stubs `<Transition>` by default; the region cases pass
  `global.stubs.transition = false`.
- `Element.prototype.matches` is overloaded in lib.dom; a patch assigned as a plain function
  needs `as typeof Element.prototype.matches`.
- The extension build regenerates `types/auto-imports.d.ts`; the hand edit was byte-identical to
  the regenerated file, so the build adds nothing to the diff.
- Biome's formatter folds a `test("…", async ({ registeredExtension }) => {` line past 120
  columns; run `biome format --write` on a new spec before `bun run lint`.
- This agent's sandbox refuses compound shell lines that contain quoted heredocs or computed
  operands; the rewrites ran as scratchpad scripts with plain arguments.
