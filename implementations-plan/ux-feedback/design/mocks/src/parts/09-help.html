Jargon with nothing to explain it

HomeSendApproval windowsSettings
Your noteOverall he felt that there could be some more tooltips or ways of informing the user about stuff.

What the code does

  • The only place Fee Juice, Private Fee Juice and sponsored fees are explained is onboarding step 04, which people skip.
  • After that, terms appear bare: "Public Juice" next to "Private Fee Juice" on home (two names for the same kind of thing, GasBalanceCard.vue), "FJ" and "L2 gas" in fee rows, "From Origin / To Destination" on send, "PXE state" and "Any contract class" in settings and approvals. "Auth-witness" is never defined.
  • The tooltip primitive exists but is hover-only and mis-positioned (item 2), and very few screens use it.

What the research says

  • "If you have space, don't use tooltips or toggletips. Just provide clear labels" (Pickering). Anything you need to finish the task belongs on the screen.
  • For the rest, a toggletip: a small ⓘ or ? that opens on click, tap or Enter and stays until dismissed. Unlike hover tooltips, it works on touch and with a keyboard (WCAG 1.4.13).
  • Rule of thumb from the survey: high stakes and frequent → say it inline; high stakes and rare → toggletip; long explanations → a glossary page.

What changes

  • One glossary.ts holds every definition; a small ? next to a term opens it. The same text shows up everywhere the term does, so copy can't drift.
  • Built on the fixed tooltip primitive (item 2): click, tap and Enter open it, Esc closes it, and it stays inside the window.
  • Also fixes the naming: "Public Juice" becomes "Public Fee Juice", to pair with "Private Fee Juice".

Draft glossary

Fee Juice (FJ)
Aztec's fee token. Every transaction pays its fee in it. It can't be sent to other people.
Public / Private Fee Juice
Two separate balances. Paying from Private Fee Juice keeps your address out of the fee; paying from public names your account on the chain.
Sponsored
Someone else, usually the app, pays the fee. Test networks only.
Private / Public
Private keeps that side of a transfer hidden. Public puts it on the chain for anyone to read.
Auth-witness
A signature that lets a contract do one specific thing for your account, such as move a token during an app's transaction.
Simulation
A dry run on this device that shows what a transaction would do, without sending it.
PXE
The part of Nulo that keeps your private data and builds your transactions, on this device.
Fee contract
A contract that pays the network fee for you and takes payment another way.

My pick: A + B, C later

B covers the choices that matter every time you send. A covers the rest without cluttering screens, and one glossary keeps the wording consistent across home, send and approvals. C is cheap once A exists, since the page is just the glossary rendered.

Confidence: moderate-high. The glossary copy is a first draft for you to edit.
