The alias tooltip runs off the window

Capabilities windowTooltip primitive (every tooltip)
Your noteOn the "Capabilities pop up" the tooltip for the alias goes outside of the width of the window size.

What the code does

  • packages/design/src/ui/Tooltip.vue places the bubble from its trigger's rectangle and never checks the window edge. With position="start" the bubble starts at the ⓘ (about 100px in) and may grow to 320px wide (calc(var(--base-width) - 40px)), so it ends around 440px inside a 400px window.
  • --base-width is 360px even inside the 400px approval windows, and nothing ever flips or shifts the bubble.
  • Two more start-aligned tooltips with long copy carry the same latent bug: the suspicious-hostname warning (DappIdentityBlock.vue:47) and the recovery-phrase hint (ImportSecretForm.vue:35).

What the research says

  • Standard collision handling is flip (other side when there's no room), then shift (slide along the edge), then size (cap width) — Floating UI. Radix shipped the same bug and fixed it the same way (radix-ui#1476).
  • Keep bubbles near 240px in the 360px popup and 260–280px in the 400px windows, under ~130 characters.
  • "Users shouldn't need to find a tooltip in order to complete their task" — NN/g. Where there's room, a visible line beats a tooltip.

What changes

  • After measuring, Tooltip.vue keeps the bubble 8px inside both window edges, flips it above/below when it doesn't fit, and caps its width at min(280px, window − 16px).
  • About fifteen lines in the one primitive; no new dependency. Fixes all three start-aligned tooltips and anything item 9 adds.

Unchanged: hover or keyboard focus shows it, leaving hides it.
