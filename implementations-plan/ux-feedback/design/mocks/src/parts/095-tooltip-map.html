The rules

  1. Two kinds only: the label of an icon-only button, or the definition of a dotted term.
  2. Never the only copy. Every definition is also in the glossary; every icon label is also the button's aria-label.
  3. Nothing clickable inside. Opens on hover and on focus; Esc closes it; the pointer can move onto it.
  4. One sentence, at most 100 characters, at most 272px wide (item 2's fix).
  5. At most two dotted terms per screen. A new one needs a glossary entry first and your sign-off in its PR.
  6. Never for a warning you must read or a choice you must make: those are visible text.

Two that already break rule 6

  • The suspicious-hostname warning in the dApp windows (DappIdentityBlock.vue) hides "some characters can imitate Latin letters" behind a hover.
  • The recovery-phrase ⓘ on import (ImportSecretForm.vue) hides that a phrase shared with another wallet lets that software derive your keys.
  • Both are security messages someone has to read, so both become visible text in this arc: a copy-and-layout change, outside what testers raised.
