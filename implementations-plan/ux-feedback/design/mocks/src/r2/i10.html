Round 2You picked A only, and the timer bar looked off. It's gone. Most systems don't draw one either; the open question is only whether successes get a close button.

What others do

  • No countdown in Material 3's snackbar or Carbon's toast (5 s by default); Sonner, notistack and react-hot-toast draw none either. react-toastify is the outlier: a bar by default (M3, Carbon, react-toastify).
  • Libraries converge on 4 to 5 seconds. Atlassian advises against auto-dismissing warnings and errors at all (Atlassian).

Why auto-hide is still fine here

  • WCAG's own example: a message that vanishes after a few seconds is acceptable when the same information is available elsewhere (2.2.1). Here it is: the History row carries the transaction and its stage.
  • Material 3 notes that auto-dismissing messages are hard for people who need more time, which is why errors stay until closed.

Success with a close button
  • One shape for every message, and you can clear a success early.
  • Costs about 24px of the text's width on every message, so long recipients get cut short sooner.

My pick: A′

With no timer drawn, the only job a × does on a success is hurry it along, and the message already leaves on its own in six seconds. Errors are the case that needs it, because they stay. Keeping the × off successes also leaves room for the recipient, which is the part people check.

Confidence: moderate. Six seconds sits above the 4 to 5 s norm on purpose, because the message carries an action.
