Round 2You picked A but aren't sold. Three ways to label it, same header, same screen. The difference that matters is whether the padlock stays.

Why the glyph is the question

  • On Home the padlock already means "private" twice (balance split, token rows), and item 8's pick puts three more on the send screen's strip.
  • A label fixes "is this a button?". It doesn't stop the same glyph from meaning two things on one screen; only dropping it does.

What others do

  • MetaMask, Phantom and 1Password all lock from a text item ("Lock", "Lock Wallet", "Lock 1Password"), not from a padlock alone (round 1's survey).

My pick: A2

If the complaint is "the lock looks like the privacy lock", the fix is to stop using the privacy lock for it. A1 and A3 answer "is it a button?" and leave the collision in place, which gets worse once the send strip carries padlocks too. Same testid and aria-label in all three; one click locks, as today.

Confidence: moderate. A2 vs A1 is the call; A3 is the fallback if the second chip looks busy in the real header.
