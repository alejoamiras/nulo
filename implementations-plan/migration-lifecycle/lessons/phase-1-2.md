# Phases 1–2 lessons — engine accounting + boot gate

- **The reject round was the valuable one.** The final fresh-context codex pass rejected revision 2 with three blockers the two conditional-approve rounds had walked past (killed-gesture + backstop autonomous terminalization; registry-maxVersion blind to code-only hotfixes; undurable backstop claim). Every blocker was implementable in small diffs. Pattern for the remaining batches: the fresh-eyes pass earns its cost precisely when the resumed rounds have converged comfortably.
- **Implementation exposed two more invariants the plan text missed**, both caught by writing the pins before declaring victory:
  1. The gate's own tests forced `gestureRuns`: with only a backstop CAP, the sequence initial→gesture→backstop still let an autonomous wake supply the terminalizing third attempt. Restricting the backstop to gesture-free episodes (autonomous runs strictly precede human ones) is what makes "terminal only by gesture" a theorem instead of a slogan.
  2. The version-invalidation test failed until invalidation ALSO cleared the engine's durable `SCHEMA_ATTEMPTS_KEY` (newly exported): voiding the verdict while inheriting an exhausted budget made the new build's first failure instantly terminal — "new build = new episode" has to mean the budget too.
- **Three engine pins were re-pinned deliberately** to the stand-down contract (crash-mid-commit now takes two boots; the crafted-backup and escaped-throw tests gained a boot) — each called out individually in the diff, none weakened: the security substance (no engine-namespace restore, load-bearing backup kept) is asserted unchanged.
- The N-27 probe pin was dropped honestly: the probe runs post-registration, unreachable without the full service graph; inspection + smoke boot logs cover a 3-line logging change. Logged as a deviation.
- Codex consults this phase: none beyond plan-stage (the two implementation-level tightenings are flagged in plan.md Phase-2 notes for the post-impl codex audit to contest).
