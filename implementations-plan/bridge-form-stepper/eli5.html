bridge-form-stepper

Right now, when you submit a bridge, two awkward things happen: a "pending" card pops up for the very transaction you're watching (duplicating the form), and the form itself goes mute — no sense of which signature is coming or where you are. This round makes the form the storyteller: submitting turns it into a live stepper that walks each phase (seal → approve → deposit → sync → claim → confirm, with skipped phases marked), ends on a receipt with both explorer links, and only hands a bridge to the Pending list when you deliberately background it or reload. The wallet connections also shrink to one row of chips with ✕-to-disconnect, and "IN-FLIGHT BRIDGES" becomes "PENDING BRIDGES".

Why this tier was chosen

mid, your call, rubric-consistent: contained UI flow on an already-audited engine, zero new trust surface — but it reshapes how the narration state flows, and the dual audit earned its fee again: both reviewers independently proved my draft's "RUN IN BACKGROUND" couldn't actually free the form (the busy flag spans the whole bridge), and the fix they converged on — all narration lives per-record in the journal's runtime, one tested mapper feeds both the stepper and the cards — is structurally better than what I drafted.

Phases

Phase 1: One narration channel + the cleanup rules

The flows stop narrating to the console and start writing their steps (sealing, approving, depositing, exiting) into the same per-bridge runtime the engine already uses — so the stepper AND the cards read one truth through one tested mapper. Plus the cleanup matrix: rejecting a wallet prompt before anything hit the chain quietly deletes the record (deposits finally behave like withdraws here); once a transaction exists, nothing is ever auto-deleted; and if the form loses track for any reason, the card always appears (fail-open).

Phase 2: The stepper and the receipt

Submitting flips the form into the phase rail — each phase says which wallet signs what, skipped phases show as skipped, failures mark the phase with an honest retry-or-background choice. "RUN IN BACKGROUND" hands the bridge to a Pending card (which can now narrate even mid-signature) and frees the form instantly. Completion shows the receipt — amount, direction, both explorer links, snapshotted so nothing can blank it — until you click NEW BRIDGE.

Phase 3: Wallet chips + the rename

Both wallet connections compress onto one row as chips — address + ✕ to disconnect — keeping all their existing behavior (the Aztec verification emojis, the wrong-network switch). The journal heading becomes "PENDING BRIDGES" with honest sub-copy: bridges this browser started but isn't actively driving.

Human context

The core idea is ownership: while the form drives a bridge, the form is its only voice — the Pending list shows nothing for it. The moment that ownership is released (you background it, you reload, something breaks), the card takes over, with the same live narration. The audits hammered the edges of that handoff: what happens if you reject the second wallet prompt (record deleted, form resets), if you reload during the receipt (a ✓ card appears — that's correct, the page lost its memory), or if the engine pauses between its checking rounds (the stepper must not flicker — phases anchor on hard facts, not transient state).
