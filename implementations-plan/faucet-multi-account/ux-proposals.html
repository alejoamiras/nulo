Multi-accountchoose one direction

The wallet can grant the faucet several accounts, but today the app silently uses the first one and hides the rest. These four proposals add a way to see, choose, and switch the active account — each rendered with the real nulo tokens (flip your theme toggle to compare dark/light). All are dApp-side only; no wallet or protocol changes.

TodayConnected chip shows one address; granted[0] is hardcoded, extra accounts dropped.
Raw materialWallet already sends alias + address per account. Every RPC takes an explicit from.

PROPOSAL DEffort · M✓ Selected direction

Choose-on-connect + dropdown

The SDK-endorsed flow: when the wallet grants more than one account, the connect sequence gains a "choose your main account" step (same grammar as the verify-emoji modal). The choice is remembered per wallet; afterwards the chip carries the quiet dropdown for switching at any time. The full lifecycle:

Decision

Proposal D was selected. The connect flow gains a choose-main-account step (only when the wallet grants more than one account); the choice is remembered per wallet and drives all three tabs (Faucet, Bridge, Fuel). Changing it later is always available through the account chip's dropdown — one click to open, one to switch, with a toast confirming the new active account (stages 3–4 above). Proposals A, B, and C remain documented for the record: A survives inside D as the switching mechanism; B's modal grammar is the fallback if the row content ever outgrows a dropdown; C was set aside because it caps out around four accounts and crowds the strip on mobile.
