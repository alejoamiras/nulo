## Medium
- The MV3 `setInterval` choice is still wrong, but the failure mode is latency, not data loss. When the SW suspends, polling stops; discovery resumes on the next worker wake. In this codebase that likely happens on the next popup open and also on the journal reaper alarm wake, because runtime boot rehydrates services and `startScheduler()` does an immediate poll. That is acceptable as a documented temporary limitation, but it is still accidental coupling to unrelated alarms.
