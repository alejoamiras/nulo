Grego — Aztec-native, infra-light

Verdict: The other shipping Aztec wallet. Stronger than Nulo on a few Aztec-protocol nuances (capability wildcards, account contract variety, fee abstraction surface), weaker on durable-job infra (no formal job system, no SW death-recovery, lighter MV3 hygiene). Useful as the comparison point for Aztec-correctness; less useful as a model for Phase 2.

Runtime shape diagram

Grego is an MV3 Aztec wallet built around a single popup + service worker + offscreen-PXE topology, similar in shape to Nulo. The key differentiators are higher in the stack: capability-based permissioning, multiple account contract artifacts, and a richer fee abstraction.

How durable jobs work here analysis

Honestly: they mostly don't. Grego shares Nulo's current limitation — no formal job registry, no SW death recovery, no chrome.alarms wake-up, no persisted progress for in-flight prove operations. The popup blocks on the prove call the same way Nulo does.

Where Grego pulls ahead is in the policy layers around the tx pipeline:

  1. Capability wildcards — authorization-manager stores permissions as wildcard patterns per origin, so a dApp can be granted read.* instead of a fixed list of accounts. A11 pattern
  2. Multiple account contracts — accounts can be created with different signing schemes (schnorr, ecdsa-k, ecdsa-r). Nulo only ships canonical schnorr today.
  3. Broader fee abstraction — fee selection covers embedded-FPC, sponsored-FPC, and fee-juice paths in a single user-facing surface.
Caveat: Grego is open-source but moves fast; some of the patterns we cataloged in the research markdown may have evolved. Verify the linked file paths in the sources section before relying on a specific structure.

Risks / anti-patterns research

RiskWhy
No durable jobsSame SW-death weakness as Nulo today. Long proofs → "wallet not responding" UX.
In-memory tx historyTx history is rebuilt from chain on each boot. Slow on cold start, lossy if chain prunes.
Lighter MV3 hygienePort lifecycle and cold-boot event handling are less defensive than MetaMask's.
Custom account contractsMultiple contract variants = larger surface area for bugs and audit cost.

What Nulo should steal later phase

  • Capability wildcards — Phase 3+. Reduces approval fatigue when Nulo opens to real users. Not Phase 2 scope.
  • Account contract registry surface — eventually, when Nulo ships beyond schnorr-only, the registry shape Grego uses is a reasonable starting model.

What not to copy phase 2

  • Lack of durable jobs — the whole point of Phase 2.
  • In-memory tx history — Nulo already deleted its custom tx-history feature in 0.11.0; any future re-add should be durable from day one.
  • Custom account contracts before they're necessary — Nulo's commitment to upstream @aztec/accounts/schnorr is a feature, not a gap.
