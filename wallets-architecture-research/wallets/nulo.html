Runtime shape diagram

Nulo is an MV3 Chromium extension. Service worker hosts business-logic services; offscreen document runs the PXE + BB-WASM stack; popup is a Vue 3 + Pinia renderer that talks to services via a typed ServiceClient<Methods, Events> over chrome.runtime.Port.

Strengths — where Nulo already wins research

  • Package discipline (★★★★★) — Biome-enforced layer rules are stricter than any of the three peers ship.
  • Cryptography hygiene (★★★★☆) — KAT-style vectors for PasswordSecretBox; KDF + encryption isolated in @nulo/wallet-crypto.
  • Aztec correctness (★★★★☆ post-PR-76) — stub-account override, completeFeeOptions parity, fast-path, embedded-FPC consolidation.
  • Cross-service compile-time deps (★★★★★) — every ServiceClient ↔ Service pair is typed end-to-end; no untyped JSON-RPC.
  • Upstream conformance — uses canonical @aztec/accounts/schnorr; no custom Noir source.

What comes next roadmap

Phase 2 = durable jobs. The full design is on the Nulo Phase 2 page. Realistic scope: 3–6 weeks of focused work, 8 items total, with items 1–3 (JobRegistry + AbortController + subscribe-by-jobId in the execute window) as the single most valuable PR.
