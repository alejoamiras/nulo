## F1: `ExecutionService` is still a multi-role facade
1. Title: `ExecutionService` is still a multi-role facade.
2. Smell name: Large Class (Fowler), with Divergent Change symptoms because transfer execution, dApp send orchestration, journal/cancel flow, gas-balance reads, fee-estimate reuse, and Aztec RPC adapter methods all change for different reasons inside one class.
3. Maintenance impact bucket: architectural; blast radius is the whole execution facade plus its extracted collaborators; change frequency is high because `service.ts` was touched in 9 commits since 2026-05-01.
4. Concrete evidence: the class is 2302 LOC; it owns 16 injected collaborators plus cache/mutex/timer state in [service.ts:252](packages/extension/src/wallet/services/execution/service.ts:252), then mixes unrelated entrypoints like [service.ts:405](packages/extension/src/wallet/services/execution/service.ts:405), [service.ts:717](packages/extension/src/wallet/services/execution/service.ts:717), [service.ts:836](packages/extension/src/wallet/services/execution/service.ts:836), [service.ts:914](packages/extension/src/wallet/services/execution/service.ts:914), [service.ts:1476](packages/extension/src/wallet/services/execution/service.ts:1476), [service.ts:1713](packages/extension/src/wallet/services/execution/service.ts:1713), [service.ts:1860](packages/extension/src/wallet/services/execution/service.ts:1860), and [service.ts:2207](packages/extension/src/wallet/services/execution/service.ts:2207).
5. Why it harms future change: a change to journal semantics, fee reuse, dApp execution ordering, or Aztec read APIs all collides in the same file, so unrelated work creates merge pressure and makes local reasoning expensive.
6. Smallest safe refactoring: Extract Class plus Move Method, keeping `ExecutionService` as a thin RPC facade over narrower executors such as transfer execution, dApp send execution, read-only Aztec RPC, and gas-balance queries.
7. What disappears: constructor/state sprawl, unrelated caches and mutexes living beside RPC endpoints, and the need to audit one 2.3k-line file for every execution-related change.
8. Instances: `service.ts:252`, `service.ts:281`, `service.ts:295`, `service.ts:317`, `service.ts:405`, `service.ts:717`, `service.ts:836`, `service.ts:914`, `service.ts:1221`, `service.ts:1285`, `service.ts:1476`, `service.ts:1713`, `service.ts:1860`, `service.ts:2022`, `service.ts:2207`, `service.ts:2265`.

## F3: Queued-journal claim and cancel rely on microtask timing
1. Title: Queued-journal claim and cancel rely on microtask timing.
2. Smell name: Temporal coupling (named close analog): the cancel flow only works if `transitionOperation`, controller registration, and controller lookup happen in a precise order across modules, but no abstraction enforces that order.
3. Maintenance impact bucket: architectural; blast radius is `claim-helper.ts` plus the cancel/acquire paths in `service.ts`; change frequency is high because `claim-helper.ts` + `service.ts` were touched in 9 commits since 2026-05-01.
4. Concrete evidence: `claimOrCreateDappExecuteJournal` transitions `queued -> pending` and then immediately registers the controller, with an explicit “correctness-by-microtask-interleaving” warning in [claim-helper.ts:145](packages/extension/src/wallet/services/execution/claim-helper.ts:145); `cancelJob` separately transitions the journal and then looks up the controller in [service.ts:836](packages/extension/src/wallet/services/execution/service.ts:836); `acquireExecutionSlot` pre-registers another controller variant before the wait in [service.ts:1302](packages/extension/src/wallet/services/execution/service.ts:1302).
5. Why it harms future change: an innocent refactor such as adding an `await`, moving logging, or centralizing controller cleanup can silently break the queued-cancel invariant because the dependency is implicit and cross-file.
6. Smallest safe refactoring: Move Function into one cancellation/claim coordinator that atomically owns journal transition plus controller registration, or lift that compound operation into `OperationJournalService`.
7. What disappears: race-sensitive comments, duplicated controller bookkeeping, and the hidden requirement that two modules preserve the same async interleaving forever.
8. Instances: `claim-helper.ts:78`, `claim-helper.ts:125`, `claim-helper.ts:145`, `claim-helper.ts:163`, `service.ts:836`, `service.ts:1285`, `service.ts:1304`.
