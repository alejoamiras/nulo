1. Title: Popup CRUD dialogs reimplement the same lifecycle glue.

3. Maintenance impact bucket: structural. Blast radius: 10 popup files plus the shared form/entity composables they partially bypass. Change frequency: low per file but family-wide and still active; each of these dialogs shows 1 commit in the last 3 months.
